About

PoliChan is a political discussion imageboard built from the smoldering remains of /pol/.

Anyone can post anonymously. Wallet-linked tripcodes and PoliPass flairs are available for posters who want to verify themselves or support the site.

PoliChan is independently run. See the Constitution for how the site is governed, or head to /meta/ to leave feedback or report a problem.
